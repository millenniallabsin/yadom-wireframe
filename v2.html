02 / The Yadom story

Meet Yadom

India doesn't come with a volume knob.So we made a switch.

Every Indian home has that one cupboard. Camphor, mint, tulsi, and whatever your grandmother swore by.

Yadom takes those rituals out of the cupboard and makes them small enough to carry, loud enough to notice, and simple enough to use without a lecture.

Our sun has four moods. So do you. Pick one, flip the switch, carry on.

Not plain. Not clinical. Not boring.
Drag to turn the Sun
BUILD Text left, Sun right (stacks on mobile: text first). The Sun floats and blinks: eyes close, open, close, open. People can drag to rotate it in 3D; it eases back when let go.
