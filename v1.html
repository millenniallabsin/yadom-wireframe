02 / The premise

Our story

India doesn't come with a volume knob. So we made a switch.

Every Indian home has that one cupboard. Camphor, mint, tulsi, and whatever your grandmother swore by. We took those rituals out of the cupboard and made them small enough to carry, loud enough to notice, and simple enough to use without a lecture.

Not plain. Not clinical. Not boring.

ANIMATION Each line stamps in like a rubber stamp hitting paper. No product on this screen; the 3D jar waits for its own section. This is where the current "classic Indian ritual" story moves up from the inhaler to the whole brand.
03 / The switch (pinned for three screens)

Pick your mood.

Same world. Same sun. Three states.

Mood 01

Relax

Wired to calm.

For when the city won't log off.

STATE The river goes still, birds hang in mid-air, cows lie down, the dinosaur naps. A soft haze settles. Eyes close, content.

Mood 02

Reset

Foggy to sharp.

For when your brain starts buffering.

STATE A cool gust blows through. The haze clears, outlines sharpen, birds get blown off course. Eyes snap wide open.

Mood 03

Rejuvenate

Drained to alive.

Like the first rain, minus the traffic.

STATE The first rain. Fields turn green, flowers open, the Sun's rays turn into petals.

HOW IT WORKS The section stays pinned for three screens of scrolling, and the same landscape changes state at each third. The matchbox toggles let people flip back and forth themselves, so the idea is something they do, not just read. States change through weather and texture only, never light, so nothing reads as morning or night. Try it: scroll through, or tap a matchbox.

Build notes For the team. Not part of the page.
WHAT THE ILLUSTRATOR MAKES
  • One base landscape: the current hero, extended so it can hold the switch
  • Three state layers on that same composition: Relax (haze, stillness), Reset (wind, crisp lines), Rejuvenate (rain, bloom)
  • Five Sun faces: peek, Relax, Reset, Rejuvenate, wink
  • Three matchbox labels and one matchbox tray
  • Three mood vignettes for the Act II openers (crops of the state layers work)
WHAT THE 3D TEAM MAKES
  • Inhaler jar: exists, keep its scroll path and cap-pop
  • Lotion and body wash (or whatever the client confirms), each with one open moment: pump press, flip cap
  • On low-end phones, swap the full 3D for a lighter turntable spin
HOW IT'S BUILT
  • Same Next.js front and Shopify back as today
  • The switch: one pinned section, the three state layers crossfade on scroll, Sun faces swap as layers. Same scroll engine the current jar uses
  • The floating Shop pill stays. It steps aside while the switch is pinned, and its colour follows the mood section you're in
  • Mobile: the switch pins the same way; the matchboxes sit as a bottom bar
RULES THAT HOLD THE IDEA TOGETHER
  • No sunrise, sunset or night light anywhere in Act I. Moods change weather, never time
  • Moods are feelings; moments can be any time of day
  • No medical claims. "How it feels", never "what it treats"
OPEN DECISIONS
  • Which products sit in which mood, and which are live at launch
  • The name for the three: Rasas, Moods or Suns
  • Act II order: keep Relax, Reset, Rejuvenate, or put Reset first while it's the only mood that sells today
  • Family pack: yes or no
  • Brand line above the three: "Same sun. Different mood."
